YOUR QUESTIONS

Got questions? We’ve got answers. Explore our FAQ to learn more about our process, pricing, and how we bring your creative vision to life.

What services does Zentro offer?

We specialize in branding, web design, UI/UX, packaging, and visual identity. Whether you're starting fresh or rebranding, we craft creative solutions that connect.

How does the design process work?

Our process includes discovery, concept development, design execution, and final delivery. We keep communication clear and collaborative every step of the way.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary based on project scope, but most branding or website projects take 2–6 weeks. We'll give you a detailed timeline before we begin.

Can I request revisions?

Absolutely. Every package includes a set number of revisions to ensure you’re happy with the final result. Additional revisions can be added if needed.

What are your pricing options?

We offer flexible pricing based on your needs. From starter packages to full-scale design systems, we tailor each quote to fit your goals and budget.

Do you work with clients outside our city or country?

Yes! Zentro works with clients worldwide. We collaborate smoothly across time zones using virtual tools and clear communication.
